About SETTLEMATE

Your New City. Your New Home. Everything in One Place.

The problem

Moving to an unfamiliar city for study or work means searching across many apps, groups and word of mouth for housing, food, healthcare and daily help.

Our idea

SETTLEMATE brings these essentials into one location-based place so newcomers can settle in faster and with more confidence.

About this prototype

This is a college project. Listings, ratings and badges are sample data, and login is a demonstration with no server.

Frequently asked questions

Are the listings real?

No. All listings are sample data for demonstration. Verify any real provider yourself.

Is my location stored?

Only in your own browser (localStorage), and only if you allow it. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Is login secure?

Login here is a frontend demo. Passwords are never stored. Real accounts need a backend.

Can I book or pay?

Bookings are saved only in your browser. There are no real payments.

What do I do in an emergency?

Call 112 immediately. See the Emergency page for helplines.
